Mote Pillo

Description

A classic Ecuadorian dish: White hominy mixed with eggs, cheese, and chopped oninos

Ingredients

  • can of white hominy
  • cage-free eggs (3 per serving)
  • shredded cheese
  • butter
  • chopped green onions
  • salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Gather Ingredients
  2. Chop green onions and have ready nearby
  3. Rinse hominy in a strainer
  4. Melt 2 tbsp of butter and toss in hominy
  5. After hominy has browned and textured to tase, throw in eggs and cook for ~2 minutes
  6. Toss in shredded cheese and chopped green onions
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!
