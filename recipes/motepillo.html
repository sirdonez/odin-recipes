Mote Pillo

Description

These creamy crockpot mashed potatoes free up stove space for other side dishes on busy holidays. They melt in your mouth and couldn't be easier to make with boiled potatoes, sour cream, garlic, and butter in your slow cooker.

Ingredients

  • red potates
  • chicken bouillon
  • minced garlic
  • sour cream
  • package cream cheese
  • butter
  • salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Gather Ingredients
  2. Place potatoes, bouillon, and garlic in a large pot and cover with water; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until tender, about 15 minutes.
  3. Drain in a colander over a large bowl, reserving cooking water
  4. Mash Potatoes until smooth and creamy.
  5. Transfer mashed potatoes to slow cooker
  6. Before serving, stir in butter and seasoning to taste
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!
