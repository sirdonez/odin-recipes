Grilled PB & J Sandwich

Description

This is peanut butter and jelly with a twist. The kids will thank you!

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp butter
  • 2 slices white bread
  • 2 tsp preferred fruit jelly
  • 1 tsp peanut butter

Steps

  1. Gather Ingredients
  2. Heat griddle or skillet to 350 F
  3. Spread butter on outside side of each slice. Then, on the inside of each slice, spread pb on one side and jelly on the other
  4. Combine slices so pb and j sides are in the middle
  5. Cook on preheated griddle/skillet until golden brown and heated through, 4 minutes on each side
